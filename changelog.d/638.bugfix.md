Fixes an interactive `codex` pane getting stuck showing "Thinking" forever: the wrapper's Codex line classifier only knew how to resolve back to `Idle` from JSON (`codex exec --json`) output, so the interactive TUI's plain-text redraw lines could drive the card to `Working` but never back down, even once the session had gone quiet. The non-JSON fallback now recognizes the interactive TUI's own composer idle placeholder ("Ask Codex to do anything") as a real idle signal, with a precedence guard against a co-resident active-turn signal ("esc to interrupt") in the same classification batch — since the composer placeholder is on screen for an entire running turn (not just at rest), the guard is what keeps the card reading `Working` during a turn instead of falsely reading `Idle` mid-work.
