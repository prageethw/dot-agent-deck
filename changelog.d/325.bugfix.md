- **Worktree removals the deck performs itself now record who/what triggered them**

  Two of the places the deck removes a worktree — `worker-agent-deck worktree reclaim` and the TUI's own self-cleanup of a half-created worktree after `git worktree add` times out — previously dropped the caller's identity on the floor entirely, leaving no trace of who removed a worktree after the fact (issue #325). A third path, `issue_dispatch_run::remove_worktree` (tab-close / scheduled cleanup), is not yet attributed and is tracked separately.

  `worker-agent-deck worktree reclaim` now resolves the caller's identity the same way `issue claim`'s lock already does (CLAUDE.md rule 23's local-signal resolution, via a diagnostic `issue_claim::resolve_remover_identity` built on top of `resolve_caller_identity` rather than duplicated), records it on every `WorktreeReport` it actually removes in a new `removed_by` field (`pending`/`kept` reports carry `None` — nothing was removed, nothing to attribute), and now prints and logs it too: `worktree reclaim`'s `Removed:` section names the remover next to each path, and a confirmed removal also logs a `tracing::info!` line to `DOT_AGENT_DECK_LOG`. The TUI's timeout self-cleanup forwards the same `creator` identity that requested the worktree in the first place — this is always a creator cleaning up its own half-created directory, so no separate identity resolution is needed — via `WorktreeCreation::TimedOut`'s field, renamed from `cleaned_up: bool` to `cleaned_up_by: Option<String>`, and now logs it too.
