Fixes `capture_bounded_async`'s five process-table remediation arms (read-error, budget-timeout, size-cap-trip and post-read wait-timeout, plus the unreachable stdout-pipe-missing guard) leaving the post-`kill()` reap unbounded — a child wedged in uninterruptible sleep or stopped by `SIGSTOP` could park the daemon's whole poll on the remediation path instead of the happy path it was already bounded on. Each arm now sends `SIGKILL` via the non-async `start_kill()` and bounds the reap with a short, fixed `KILL_REAP_TIMEOUT` (500ms) independent of the overall sample budget; `kill_on_drop(true)` remains the safety net if even that bounded wait expires, so no arm can either park indefinitely or leave a permanent zombie.
