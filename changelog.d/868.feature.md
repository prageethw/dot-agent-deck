## The inbox line sits after the worker's name on a card

A session card in the TUI used to draw its inbox line (`✉2 queued`, `✉ delivered`, `✉ delivery uncertain` and the others) in the top-right chip beside the status. The title, which holds the shortcut number and, with the agent badge on, the agent and model such as `ClaudeCode (opus-4.7)`, gets only what that chip leaves, so it was cut with `…` as soon as a message had been delivered, even though the worker's name sits on its own row below with room to spare.

`worker-agent-deck` now draws the line on that name row, two spaces after the name, and the top-right chip carries the status alone, so the title keeps the whole width it has when the inbox is empty. The words are unchanged. The name keeps its room: where the row cannot hold the name, the gap and the whole line, the line shortens to the pending count (`✉2`), and where even that does not fit it is left out. On a three-row card, whose one row also carries `Dir:`, the order is the name, the inbox line, then `Dir:`, and each is dropped before the one to its left; a Blocked or Orphaned reason that takes `Dir:`'s place outranks the inbox line there. The name is never shortened to make room, so a very long pane name can hide the line. Cards one and two rows tall still draw no inbox line (issue #868).
