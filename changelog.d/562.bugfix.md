Fixes `worker-agent-deck`'s pane title and tool line rendering a raw control byte or Unicode bidi override (e.g. `U+202E` RIGHT-TO-LEFT OVERRIDE) sent through two seams that PR #558's display_name fix scoped out: an agent's `display_name` reaching `ui.display_names` unsanitized on hydration and rename, and a `ToolStart` event's `tool_name`/`tool_detail` reaching the rendered tool line unsanitized via the direct hook-socket ingest path. `display_name` is now scrubbed and length-clamped at the daemon-echo boundary and the rename gate now rejects Unicode format characters as well as raw control bytes; `tool_name`/`tool_detail` are now scrubbed and length-clamped at `ToolStart` ingest, matching the treatment already applied on the hydration path.
