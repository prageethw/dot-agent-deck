## A forged "waiting for you" status could no longer be told apart from a real one

A status planted on a pane by an unidentified producer could be silently promoted to a trusted one. worker-agent-deck tracks, per pane, whether a pending-permission marker is currently set — a plain `WaitingForInput` always clears that marker, and a tagged `ToolStart` treated a missing marker as proof that the waiting prompt it was now looking at must be its own. That heuristic didn't check who had set the status being looked at: an unidentified producer could plant a forged "waiting for you" status on a pane, and the moment the pane's genuine agent did anything at all — emitting its own `ToolStart` — that tag treated the forged status as self-authored and left it in place, laundering it into something the deck now trusted.

The deck now only applies that heuristic when the current status came from an identified producer. A status of unknown provenance no longer gets treated as trustworthy just because the pane's own agent later touched it.
