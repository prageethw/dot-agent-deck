## A worktree whose spawn-rollback cleanup fails is no longer dropped from the registry

`handle_dispatch`'s spawn-rollback path (`src/dispatch.rs`), for the shared-checkout arm, discarded `remove_worktree`'s outcome (`let _ = remove_worktree(...)`) and then unconditionally dropped the worktree from `ctx.worktrees` regardless of whether the removal actually succeeded. When `git worktree remove --force` genuinely failed — e.g. the worktree was locked — the directory stayed on disk but its registry entry was gone, losing the only record that it still existed there.

The outcome is now matched instead of discarded: the registry entry is only dropped when `remove_worktree` reports `RemoveOutcome::Removed`, and `cleanup_failed` (which drives the warning log and the user-facing "cleanup failed" note) now also reflects a failed worktree removal, not only a failed `branch -D`.
