## The TUI No Longer Draws an ORCHESTRATOR Badge on the Orchestrator's Card

The orchestration tab's orchestrator card no longer carries the `ORCHESTRATOR` word that v0.51.0 added. The card already names the role and the orchestrator's tab is highlighted, so the word said the same thing a second time, and on the narrow sidebar it took a row of its own that the card's prompt, tool and `Dir:` rows had to give up. Those rows are back, and the orchestrator's card now looks like every other role's card.

Nothing else about finding and messaging an orchestrator changes. `worker-agent-deck roles` still marks each orchestration's orchestrator in its `ORCHESTRATOR` column, `inbox send` still reaches it, and the desktop app still badges it. An orchestration whose seat role is not named `orchestrator` is now told apart in the TUI by its role name and the highlighted tab alone.
