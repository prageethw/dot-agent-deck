## Orchestration Tabs Return Focus to the Orchestrator on Their Own

In an orchestration tab, focus stayed wherever you last left it. After glancing at a worker pane you had to walk focus back to the orchestrator yourself before typing your next command — and it was easy to forget, so the next thing you typed went to a pane you did not mean.

Orchestration tabs now bring focus back to the orchestrator pane on their own in two situations. The moment the last role pane that was waiting for input resolves — so nothing on the tab still needs you — focus moves to the orchestrator once, not repeatedly every frame. And if you move focus to another role pane yourself and then leave it untouched for 30 seconds, focus snaps back to the orchestrator. Anything you do in that pane restarts the 30 seconds, and the snap-back holds off entirely while any role on the tab is still waiting for input, so you are never pulled away from a pane that is asking you a question.

Both behaviors are limited to orchestration tabs — the Dashboard and workspace-mode tabs never move focus on their own. One gap remains for now: a keystroke that the `Ctrl+e` command-entry lock drops rather than forwards does not yet count as activity, so typing at a locked role pane can still look idle to the 30-second timer.
