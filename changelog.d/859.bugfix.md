## The Orchestrator's Card in the TUI Is Drawn Like Every Other Card Again

v0.51.0 started drawing an `ORCHESTRATOR` word on the orchestrator's card in an orchestration tab's sidebar: at the end of the title row where it fitted, and on a row of its own at the bottom of the card where it did not. `worker-agent-deck` does not draw it. The card already names the role, so the word said the same thing a second time, and on the narrow sidebar its row pushed out one the card otherwise shows, the `Prmt:` row at Compact density and the `Dir:` line at Minimal density. The orchestrator's card is drawn as it was in v0.50.4 again: it has the same rows as any other role's card, in the same order and at the same width.

Nothing else about finding and messaging an orchestrator changes. `worker-agent-deck roles` still marks each orchestration's orchestrator in its `ORCHESTRATOR` column, `inbox send` still reaches it, and the desktop app still badges it. In the TUI, `roles` is where to look when you need to tell which agent an orchestration's orchestrator is, since its card now shows only its role name (issue #859).
