## Worker panes are sized equally

Worker panes that share a column were not always the same height: the leftover rows from the division could land on middle panes, so a 3-pane column of 10 rows came out 3, 4, 3. The column height is now divided in whole rows so that pane heights differ by at most one row, and any leftover rows go to the first panes (4, 3, 3).
