Closed the last way a pane could read **Working** forever after you reconnected to the deck. The daemon promotes a pane to `Working` on its own while a foreground shell command runs and demotes it when the command finishes, and it announces each of those exactly once. On reconnect the TUI used to ask the daemon for its agent list and open its event stream at the same time, in no particular order — so if the command happened to finish in between, the "finished" announcement was sent before the TUI was listening and was simply gone. The card was rebuilt from a snapshot that was already out of date, and nothing was left to correct it.

Reconnect now opens the event stream first and waits until the daemon has confirmed it before asking for the agent list, and anything that arrives while the cards are still being rebuilt is held and applied once they exist. An event that lands in that window now reaches the card it belongs to instead of being dropped.
