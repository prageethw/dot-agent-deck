Fixed a card getting stuck on **WaitingForInput** ("Needs Input") after its permission prompt was approved. Approving a prompt writes the reply straight into the agent's pane rather than updating the card's status itself, so the status only cleared once *some* tool finished — not necessarily the one that had just been approved — leaving the card reading "Needs Input" for the whole duration of a now-running, human-approved tool.

The approved tool's own start now clears the status immediately, while a concurrent, unrelated tool starting on the same pane (e.g. a subagent) still correctly leaves a genuinely outstanding "Needs Input" alone.
