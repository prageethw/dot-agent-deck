## A worker that has reported `work-done` no longer keeps reading `Idle (delegated)` or gets reported by the idle-worker detector as not having responded

When an orchestrator re-delegated to a worker pane before that worker had answered, the daemon counted the older delegation as a debt still owed, and a single `work-done` paid off that debt instead of retiring the delegation it actually answered. The newest record stayed armed over work that was already finished: the worker's card kept reading `Idle (delegated)`, and the idle-worker detector could later report a worker that had completed its task. The debt carried into the next delegation too, so the misfiling repeated every cycle. A `work-done` in `worker-agent-deck` now retires the pane's whole outstanding-delegation record, older generations included. The trade-off is that a worker which was re-delegated before answering and then stalls on the newer task goes unreported until the next delegate to that pane arms a fresh watch. Silent-worker (undelivered-pointer) accounting is unchanged. Ports upstream vfarcic/dot-agent-deck#1125 (issue #849).
