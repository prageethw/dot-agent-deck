## Tests: the kept-worktree preview probe-timeout test no longer races a 1-nanosecond deadline

`kept_worktree_preview_still_reports_the_path_when_the_probe_times_out` gave the probe a `Duration::from_nanos(1)` deadline and asserted that it timed out, so it failed whenever the probe happened to finish first. The timeout is now produced deterministically. No product behaviour changes.
