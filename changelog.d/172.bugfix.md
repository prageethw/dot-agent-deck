A dispatched triage agent's own `gh` writes now leave a record behind of what it was asked to do (issue #172). When an `issue_dispatch` task has `triage = true`, every dispatched issue's prompt still carries the triage instruction exactly as before, but the deck now also writes one audit line to `deck.log` naming the repo, the issue number, a timestamp, and the instruction template itself — never the issue's own body or comments, which stay untrusted third-party text and are deliberately kept out of the audit record so it can't become a second injection/leak sink.

This closes half of the gap raised by the security audit of PRD #421 (finding A11/F11): the deck still deliberately never parses a triage agent's response (that decision is unchanged and remains correct), but a triage-authorised `gh` write is now at least traceable after the fact against what the deck actually told the agent to do. Narrowing what the triage instruction itself grants — the other half of #172's proposal — is explicitly out of scope for this fix.

This is an internal audit-log mechanism (a `tracing::info!` line), not TUI-observable/catalog-tracked scenario behavior, so retagging with `#[spec(...)]` is out of scope for this change.
