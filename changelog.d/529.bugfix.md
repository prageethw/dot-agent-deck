Fixes a responsiveness regression in detached spawn-prompt delivery introduced as an unintended side effect of PR #525 (fix for issue #422 item 2). `confirm_prompt_delivery`'s retry loop (`src/spawn.rs`) used one shared window, `unconfirmed_retry_delay`, for two logically distinct waits: the backoff between retries once a delivery is confirmed ARMED (correctly slowed from 500ms to 10s/15s by #525, to stop racing a genuine late TEXT confirmation), and the poll while still UNARMED, before any producer has even reported itself — which got slowed down too, with nothing to race while unarmed. A producer that reported itself as little as ~500ms late now took up to 10 seconds to recover instead of near-instant.

The two waits are now split. A new `unarmed_poll_interval()` (`src/prompt_delivery.rs`) governs the loop while unarmed: a short, fixed 500ms interval, restoring the pre-#525 responsiveness for this case. `unconfirmed_retry_delay`'s escalating 10s/15s schedule is unchanged and continues to apply once a delivery is armed.

Pinned by an extension to `dispatch_016_detached_retry_stops_before_replacement_or_clear`'s existing deck-spawned/#570 sub-case (`src/spawn.rs`), which now temporarily inflates the armed-backoff schedule's own test-only override to 4s around itself and confirms the write still lands on the unarmed poll's ~500ms timescale — proving the two windows are actually decoupled, not merely both short under today's test configuration.
