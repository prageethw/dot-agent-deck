## The Orchestrator Sidebar's Cards Fill the Column Again

When the sidebar had more than three rows for each card but fewer than four, such as six roles in a 25-row terminal, `worker-agent-deck` drew every card three rows tall and left a blank band above the stats bar. The previous release spread those spare rows over the cards instead (four, four, four, three, three, three) and filled the column. The same happened with seven, eight and eleven roles at the matching heights, and with two card columns.

The spare rows are dealt to the first cards again, one row each, so the column runs down to the stats bar with no blank band. A card that is three rows tall keeps its compact form, with the role and `Dir:` on one row, and a card given four or more rows keeps `Dir:` on a row of its own. Where the rows are exactly three per card, or four or more per card, nothing changes.
