## A 3-row deck card now shows its working directory

When the grid squeezed a session card down to exactly three rows (border, one inner row, border), the card showed only the role name and the `Dir:` line was clipped away, so you could not tell where that agent was running. The single inner row now reads `<role>  Dir: <directory name>`: the role keeps priority, and `Dir:` takes whatever width remains, ending in `…` when the name is too long. Cards of four or more rows, and the one- and two-row forms, are unchanged (issue #847).
