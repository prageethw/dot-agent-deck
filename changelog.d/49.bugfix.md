Fixed a card stranded on a stale status after the deck lost its connection to the daemon mid-session. The TUI keeps one long-lived event stream open for everything the daemon reports, and it reconnects on its own when that stream dies — a daemon restart, a dropped socket, or the daemon closing a stream that fell too far behind. It re-opened the stream but never re-read the daemon's actual state, so anything that happened during the outage was simply gone: a pane that finished its work while the stream was down kept showing **Working** indefinitely, with nothing left to correct it.

Reconnecting now re-reads the daemon's current state and reconciles the cards already on screen against it, so a status that changed during the outage is recovered on the next reconnect. This happens on every reconnect regardless of why the stream ended, since a connection that simply drops loses just as much as one that announces itself. It only updates cards that already exist — no pane is created, re-attached, or duplicated by the recovery — and events arriving on the new stream are applied on top of the refreshed state, never underneath it.
