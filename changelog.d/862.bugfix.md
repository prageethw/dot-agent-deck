## The Cross-Version Check Types Into Worker Panes Again

The `cross-version` CI check (`cargo xver`, which runs the previous release's daemon against this branch's TUI and the other way round) pressed the TUI's `Ctrl+D` then `Ctrl+E` unlock before typing into the `coder` and `reviewer` panes only when it ran with `--experimental`. `worker-agent-deck` has no experimental flag in front of the command-entry lock: the lock is always on, so a default run had every keystroke refused with `Pane locked`, and its tells 3 and 4 (a delegate is still routed, `work-done` and status still arrive) failed in both directions whatever the two builds did. The check now unlocks worker-pane entry in every run, `--experimental` or not, so those two tells judge the two builds instead of the lock (issue #862).

No-Test: pinned by `inner::tests::the_worker_unlock_does_not_depend_on_the_experimental_flag` in `xtask/cross-version/src/inner.rs` (RED before this fix, GREEN after), with `the_worker_unlock_is_still_wired_in_before_the_first_worker_command` as its control. Both are plain `#[test]`s in a dev-tooling crate, matching every other test in that file (none carry `#[spec(...)]`), so R3's spec-test-delta scan does not see them. The harness is not TUI-observable catalog behavior, and the real proof is the `cross-version` CI job reading `[PASS] tell-3` and `[PASS] tell-4`, so retagging them with `#[spec(...)]` is out of scope.
