## Worktrees the deck creates now record who created them

`dot-agent-deck-owner` marked a worktree as one the deck made, but not which task or orchestration made it. When `reclaim` found a worktree it couldn't prove was safe to remove, the marker had nothing more to say than "a deck made this" — not enough to tell whose work was still sitting there, or who to ask before removing it.

Every worktree the deck creates — a task's issue-dispatch clone, or an orchestration's Spawn Pane — now records the creator's identity alongside the marker: `issue-dispatch:<task>#<issue>` for a dispatched task, `orchestration:<name>` for an orchestration-created worktree. A later `reclaim` can use that identity to say whose worktree it's asking about, instead of only that some deck made it.

This does not change how existing worktrees are recognized. A worktree marked by an older build, or one the deck never marked at all, is not retroactively claimed with an identity — it still stays foreign, and `reclaim` still asks about it rather than assume ownership it can't prove.
